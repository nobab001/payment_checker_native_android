# PayCheck Brand & Style Guide

## Brand Color Palette

We utilize a high-quality indigo and bright electric blue gradient for our visual identity.

- **Primary Color**: `#1A237E` (Dark Royal Indigo - used as theme primary)
- **Gradient Start (Top)**: `#00B4FF` (Bright Cyan)
- **Gradient End (Bottom)**: `#004BFF` (Electric Blue)

---

## Logo Background Usage Rules

- **Light Backgrounds**: Always use the full-color linear gradient version.
- **Dark/Colored Backgrounds**: Use the solid white outline logo or inverse white vector to ensure contrast.
- **System Tray/Notifications**: Only use the monochrome white outline outline (`logo-monochrome.svg`).

---

## Forbidden Usages (কঠোরভাবে নিষিদ্ধ)

To maintain brand consistency, the following modifications to the branding assets are strictly forbidden:

1. ❌ **Do not rotate** the shield or the checkmark.
2. ❌ **Do not stretch**, compress, or distort the aspect ratio of the logo.
3. ❌ **Do not add drop shadows** or outer glows directly to the vector paths.
4. ❌ **Do not modify the color gradients** or use unapproved color sets.
5. ❌ **Do not separate** the checkmark from the shield boundary.
6. ❌ **Do not merge** the logo with other app badges, marks, or user photos.
